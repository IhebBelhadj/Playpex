The server keeps everything it downloads, as a torrent client does. Open **Downloads** on the dashboard.

## The list

Each download shows its state (downloading, seeding, paused, queued, completed), its progress, speeds and peers. Select one for its details: General, Files (with a priority for each), Peers, Trackers, Pieces and Speed.

## What you can do

- **Add torrent**: paste a magnet link or upload a `.torrent` file.
- Pause, resume, force start, and move items up or down the queue.
- Set a seeding goal (a ratio or a time) and speed limits, for the server or per download.
- Add or remove trackers, reannounce, recheck the pieces, and export the `.torrent`.
- **Pause all downloads** with one switch.

## Kept, never lost

A torrent that a screen played stays as a download. Leaving the player pauses it, 30 seconds after the last screen lets go; nothing is deleted unless you remove it.

## Direct downloads

Search accepts a link: paste the address of a page or a video and the server lists what it holds (videos, playlists, magnet links, `.torrent` files) and downloads over HTTP, resuming where it stopped.

## Engines

The built-in engine is torrent-stream. **Settings › Downloads** offers two optional engines, rqbit and Playpex's own torrentd, installed from there; choose one and restart the server.

Downloading copyrighted content may be illegal where you live: you are responsible for what you add.
