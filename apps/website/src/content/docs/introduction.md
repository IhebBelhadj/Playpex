Playpex keeps your films and series on a computer in your home and plays them on every screen on your Wi-Fi: your TV, your phone and any browser.

It is made of one server and the apps that play from it:

| App | What it does | Runs on |
|---|---|---|
| **Playpex Media Server** | Keeps your films and series, finds their posters, downloads, and streams to every screen. You run it from its dashboard in a browser. | Windows, macOS, Linux |
| **Playpex TV** | The big-screen app for a computer plugged into your TV, driven by a remote: arrows, OK and Back. | Windows, macOS, Linux |
| **Playpex Mobile** | Your library on your phone, every file played as it is. | Phones and tablets |
| **Server Manager** | Finds your server on the Wi-Fi and opens its dashboard on your phone, with the remote for your TV. | Phones |

Everything lives on the server: your library, profiles, watch lists and the minute you stopped at. A screen only shows it, so you can stop a film on the TV and finish it on your phone.

## Where to start

1. [Install the server](install-the-server) on the computer that keeps your films.
2. [Add your library](add-your-library): point it at your folders.
3. [Connect a screen](connect-a-screen): Playpex TV, Playpex Mobile or a browser.

Writing a plugin? Start with [Plugins for developers](plugins-overview).
