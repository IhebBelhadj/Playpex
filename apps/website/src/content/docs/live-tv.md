Live TV comes from plugins. The **Live TV (M3U)** plugin, in the official repository, plays the channels of your own M3U playlist with their XMLTV programme guide.

## Set it up

1. On the dashboard, open **Plugins › Browse** and install **Live TV (M3U)**.
2. In its **Settings**, enter your playlist's address, and your guide's address if you have one.
3. The channels appear in **Plugins › Live TV (M3U) › Channels**; press **Refresh** after changing the playlist.

## Watching

- Playpex TV gets a **Live TV** section: a guide with your channels as lines and their programmes over the next three hours. OK on what's on plays the channel.
- In the player, up and down flip channels; a banner shows what's on now and next, with a small guide.
- Search has a chip for your channels.

At most two live streams play at once by default (**Settings › Streaming**).
