Playpex Mobile plays the films and series on your server on your phone and tablet.

## What's in it

- **Home**: trending films and series, Continue watching with the minutes left, and rows picked from what you watch.
- **Movies** and **Shows**: the catalog by popularity, trend and genre.
- **My list**: what you started and what you saved.
- **Search**: films, series and people as you type.
- A page for every film and series: story, cast, ratings, every episode, and Play.

## Playing on the phone

The player decodes every container, codec and subtitle format on the phone itself, so files play as they are and the server converts nothing. Turn the phone sideways for the full-screen player.

- **Audio & subtitles** switches every track of the file, or finds subtitles online, without stopping.
- **Resume** picks up at the minute you stopped, on this phone or any other screen.
- Ten seconds back or forward, and pause, are always one tap away.

## Switching server or profile

Tap your profile at the top right for the profile's settings, **Switch profile**, and **Change server**.
