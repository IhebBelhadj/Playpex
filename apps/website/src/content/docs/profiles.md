Profiles are the people on the **Who's watching?** screen. A server holds up to 8.

## What a profile keeps

- Its watch list, history and ratings.
- The minute it stopped at in every title, on any screen.
- Its settings: the audio language and subtitles played first, playing the next episode, and how Play chooses a source.

## Kids profiles and PINs

A **kids** profile sees only titles for children.

Lock a grown-up's profile with a **PIN**: it's asked before the profile opens on any screen.

## Managing profiles

Add, edit and remove profiles from Playpex TV's **Manage profiles**, or from the dashboard's **Profiles** page.

## Profiles and plugins

A plugin that sends what you watch somewhere (Trakt, for example) only sees the profiles the admin switched on for it: dashboard **Plugins › the plugin › Profiles**.
