The server can drive the screens connected to it.

## Pause and stop from the dashboard

**Activity** lists what every screen is playing, how far it got and where it reads from. **Pause** and **Stop** act on that screen at once; Stop asks the screen first and cuts it after five seconds.

## A remote for any screen

**Remote control** lists the screens you can drive. Press **Control** on one for:

- a **touchpad**: drag to move the pointer, tap to click, long press to go back, two fingers to scroll;
- the **arrows**, OK and Back;
- the **keyboard**, to type in search;
- pause and skip, always at hand.

On a phone, [Server Manager](server-manager) gives you the same remote. The screen shows a badge and the pointer while it's being controlled.

## Allowing it on a screen

Playpex TV accepts the remote only while **Settings › Remote control › Allow remote control from the server** is on. The remote drives the Playpex window only, never the rest of the computer.
