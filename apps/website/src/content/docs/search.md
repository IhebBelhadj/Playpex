The dashboard's **Search** is one field for everything the server can reach:

- **On this server**: files in your library and your downloads.
- **Movies & shows**: titles from the catalog.
- **Streams & files**: what your plugins offer for those words.
- **Torrents**: from the plugins that list releases.
- **Live TV**: channels, when a live TV plugin is installed.

Names match however they're spelt: `Breaking.Bad`, `breaking bad` and `Breaking Bad (2008)` find the same thing.

## Pasting a link

Paste a web address instead of words and the server reads that page: the videos, playlists, magnet links and `.torrent` files on it, ready to play or download. Only public addresses are read.

## On the TV and phone

Playpex TV and Playpex Mobile search titles, people, videos and links too, with plugins adding their own chips to the results.
